I bought a 3D printer during the first wave of Covid, initially to print face shields for frontline workers. Since I was at home with a lot of time on my hands and not a lot to do, I quickly got way too into 3D printing and designing.

It was a [Creality Ender 3 V1](https://www.creality.com/products/ender-3), with no automatic bed leveller, so I spent a lot of time getting the bed level and figuring things out. Somehow, I managed to get some really detailed prints out of that old thing. I also played around with exposing infill, changing colour midway through prints, editing a bit of G-code, sanding, filling, painting, and generally seeing how much I could get away with on a single-nozzle printer.

I also printed face shields for frontline workers; [I wrote a little about that here]({{< relref "posts/3dprintingFaceShields.md" >}}).

Here are some of the things I made (and some of the experiments I got carried away with):

## A few design experiments

{{< print-project title="Arduino Uno case" images="/images/3Dprinting/3dprintingRoll34.jpg" >}}
This was actually quite fun and a bit challenging. I used Fusion 360 to design a custom case for an Arduino Uno. The board wasn't quite according to spec, so there was a lot of measuring, printing, adjusting, and making it again.
{{< /print-project >}}

{{< print-project title="Penrose triangle coaster" images="/images/3Dprinting/3dprintingRoll31.jpg" >}}
One of my first attempts at changing colour midway through a print, and playing with exposed infill patterns. The centre is a gyroid infill, printed in translucent PLA so light can shine through. I was surprised by what you can do with a single-nozzle printer and a few G-code edits.
{{< /print-project >}}

{{< print-project title="Coasters for a friend's company" images="/images/3Dprinting/3dprintingRoll32.jpg" >}}
Designed and printed some coasters for my friend's company, playing with exposed infill patterns.
{{< /print-project >}}

{{< print-project title="PyCon India 2020 keychain" images="/images/3Dprinting/3dprintingRoll18.jpg|/images/3Dprinting/3dprintingRoll24.jpg|/images/3Dprinting/3dprintingRoll25.jpg" >}}
PyCon India 2020 went online, and everyone knows the best part of a FOSS conference is the swag xD. I designed a little keychain based on the logo in Fusion 360. I didn't have red and gold filament, so I asked my friend Kristoff Braganza to print it for me. He's also an amazing photographer, and took these cool photos of the finished thing.
{{< /print-project >}}

{{< print-project title="Persian jali earrings" images="/images/3Dprinting/3dprintingRoll27.jpg" >}}
Some earrings I designed based on Persian jali patterns. This was inspired by visits to the Red Fort and Old Fort.
{{< /print-project >}}
